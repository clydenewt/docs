## Native Financial Infrastructure

  <Card title="Integrated Orderbook" icon="arrows-rotate" iconType="duotone">

  </Card>
  <Card title="High-Frequency Oracle" icon="chart-line" iconType="duotone">

  </Card>

  </Card>
  <Card title="Interchain Capabilities" icon="link" iconType="duotone">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>
