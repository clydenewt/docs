## What's New in Phase 3

Phase 3 continues the BTCFi campaign with refined multipliers and a streamlined set of eligible positions focused on the highest-impact strategies.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Looping Multiplier Increased to 2x">
    Leverage looping positions now earn a **2x multiplier** on equity (collateral minus debt), up from 1x. This makes looping strategies more rewarding for users who want to maximize their BTC exposure.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streamlined Eligible Positions">
    Phase 3 focuses incentives on core strategies:
    - **Astroport**: Only wBTC/maxBTC and maxBTC/USDC LP positions remain eligible
    - **Mars**: wBTC lending is no longer part of the BTCFi campaign
    - **Core protocols**: Amber lending, Supervaults, and looping remain the primary venues
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="LST Position Multipliers Adjusted">
    LST-based positions (uniBTC, solvBTC, eBTC) now earn 1x multipliers, aligning incentives with core BTC strategies while still rewarding participation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Phase 3 Boost Mechanics

Max boost continues to require only a **1-year lock** (introduced in Phase 2).

| Lock Duration | Max Boost Multiplier |
| ------------- | -------------------- |
| 1 year | 3x |

**Boost Formula:**
$$
\text{Boost} = \min(2, \frac{\text{NTRN}_{\text{Locked}}}{\text{TVL}_{\text{Deposited}}} \times \frac{\text{Duration}_{\text{user}}}{\text{Duration}_{\text{max (1 year)}}} \times 2)
$$

For detailed boost examples, see [Rewards Guide](/btcfi/rewards#ntrn-stake--lock-boost).
