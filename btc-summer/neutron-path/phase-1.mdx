## Eligible Protocols

### Amber Protocol (AmberFi)

#### wBTC Lending on Amber
**Eligible positions**: wBTC lending positions
**Reward multiplier**: 3x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

#### BTC LST Lending on Amber  
**Eligible positions**: BTC LST lending (eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC, LBTC)
**Reward multiplier**: 2x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

**Access**:
- **Deposit**: [app.amberfi.io/](https://app.amberfi.io/)
- **Strategies**: [app.amberfi.io/strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies)

### Supervaults

#### maxBTC Supervaults
**Eligible positions**: maxBTC paired with various assets (wBTC, USDC, LSTs)
**Reward multiplier**: 3x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

#### BTC LST Supervaults
**Eligible positions**: BTC LST pairs (eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC, LBTC with wBTC or maxBTC)
**Reward multiplier**: 2x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

**Access**: Through [app.neutron.org/supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults) or Neutron app interfaces

### Mars Protocol

**Planned eligibility**:
- Cross-collateralized perpetuals positions
- maxBTC margin trading
- BTC derivative collateral strategies

**Timeline**: Updates will be provided as integration progresses

## Supported Assets

### Primary BTC Derivatives
- **wBTC**: Wrapped Bitcoin (most liquid)
- **maxBTC**: Structured Protocol's yield-bearing BTC
- **LBTC**: Lombard Staked Bitcoin
- **solvBTC**: Solv Protocol Bitcoin
- **eBTC**: eBTC Protocol derivative
- **pumpBTC**: Pump Protocol Bitcoin
- **uniBTC**: Bedrock uniBTC

### Base Assets
- **USDC**: For maxBTC-USDC LP strategies
- **ETH**: For certain LP pairs (when available)


## Monitoring Your Positions

### Campaign Rewards
- **Dashboard**: Track points and projected rewards at [app.neutron.org/balance](https://app.neutron.org/balance)
- **Calculations**: Updated daily based on position values
- **Boost status**: Verify NTRN locking boost is properly applied

### Protocol Yields
- **Amber positions**: Monitor through [app.amberfi.io/](https://app.amberfi.io/)
- **Supervault positions**: Track through Neutron DeFi interfaces
- **Combined view**: Some interfaces may show total yields
