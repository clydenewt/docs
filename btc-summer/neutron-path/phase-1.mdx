## Eligible Protocols

### Amber Protocol (AmberFi)
**Status**: Active and eligible for rewards

**Eligible positions**:
- **BTC derivative lending**: All supported BTC LSTs
- **Leveraged strategies**: Up to 10x leverage positions
- **Basis trading**: maxBTC collateral strategies

**Access**:
- **Deposit**: [app.amberfi.io/](https://app.amberfi.io/)
- **Strategies**: [app.amberfi.io/strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies)

**Reward multiplier**: 1x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

### Supervaults
**Status**: Active and eligible for rewards

**Eligible positions**:
- **BTC derivative LP**: All supported BTC LST pairs
- **maxBTC LP**: maxBTC paired with various assets
- **Automated strategies**: All Supervault BTC-related pools

**Access**: Through Neutron app or direct Supervault interfaces

**Reward multiplier**: 1x base (eligible for NTRN locking boosts)

### Mars Protocol
**Status**: ⏳ Integration details TBD

**Planned eligibility**:
- Cross-collateralized perpetuals positions
- maxBTC margin trading
- BTC derivative collateral strategies

**Timeline**: Updates will be provided as integration progresses

## Supported Assets

### Primary BTC Derivatives
- **wBTC**: Wrapped Bitcoin (most liquid)
- **maxBTC**: Structured Protocol's yield-bearing BTC
- **LBTC**: Lombard Staked Bitcoin
- **solvBTC**: Solv Protocol Bitcoin
- **eBTC**: eBTC Protocol derivative
- **pumpBTC**: Pump Protocol Bitcoin
- **uniBTC**: Bedrock uniBTC

### Base Assets
- **USDC**: For maxBTC-USDC LP strategies
- **ETH**: For certain LP pairs (when available)

## Direct Deposit Process

### For Amber Protocol

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit AmberFi">
    Go to [app.amberfi.io/](https://app.amberfi.io/) and connect your Neutron wallet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Asset">
    Choose from available BTC derivatives for lending or leverage
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Strategy">
    Pick lending, leveraged looping, or basis trading (if available)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Deposit">
    Execute transaction and confirm position
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Rewards">
    Track both Amber yields and BTC Summer campaign points
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For Supervaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Supervaults">
    Visit Neutron app or direct Supervault interfaces
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pool">

  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Strategy">
    Understand automated market-making parameters
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Liquidity">
    Deposit assets to chosen Supervault strategy
  </Step>

    Receive trading fees + campaign incentives
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Phase 1 Reward Specifics

### Base Rewards
- **Budget allocation**: $250,000 distributed pro-rata
- **Calculation**: Based on USD value × days staked
- **Eligible positions**: All Amber and Supervault BTC-related positions

### Boost Opportunities
- **NTRN locking**: Up to 2x boost multiplier (3x total rewards)
- **Address consistency**: Boost your Neutron deposit address
- **No cross-chain complexity**: Direct boost activation

### Additional Yields
- **Amber lending**: Variable rates based on utilization
- **Supervault fees**: Trading fees from automated market making
- **Asset issuer rewards**: Potential additional rewards from LST protocols

## Strategy Recommendations

<Warning>
The following are general considerations, not financial advice (NFA). Always do your own research.
</Warning>

### Conservative Approach
- **Focus**: Amber lending positions
- **Assets**: wBTC, established LSTs
- **Risk**: Lower (lending-focused)
- **Boost**: Consider NTRN locking for enhanced rewards

### Balanced Approach  
- **Focus**: Mix of Amber lending + Supervault LP
- **Assets**: Diversify across multiple BTC derivatives
- **Risk**: Moderate (lending + LP exposure)
- **Boost**: NTRN locking recommended for optimization

### Advanced Approach
- **Focus**: Leveraged strategies on Amber
- **Assets**: maxBTC for basis trading potential
- **Risk**: Higher (leverage, liquidation risk)
- **Boost**: Maximum NTRN locking for highest multipliers
