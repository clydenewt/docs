## Campaign Basics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is BTC Summer and how does it work?">
    Bitcoin Summer is a campaign designed to jumpstart bitcoin finance on Neutron - turning BTC into a productive asset on-chain capable of offering real yield beyond price appreciation. 
    
    Users can participate by depositing into campaign vaults or eligible DeFi positions to earn:
    - **NTRN incentives** from the $10M campaign budget
    - **Partner airdrops** from asset issuers like Lombard, Solv, EtherFi
    - **DeFi yield** from underlying protocol activities
    
    At the end of each epoch (2 months), users can claim rewards or lock them for boosted future rewards.
    
    Read more: [Campaign Details](/btc-summer/campaign-details)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much can I earn?">
    Rewards depend on:
    - **Deposit size**: Larger deposits earn more points
    - **Duration**: Full epoch participation maximizes rewards  
    - **Boost mechanisms**: NTRN locking can triple your rewards (up to 3x total)
    - **Epoch budget**: Epoch 1 has $250,000, later epochs will be larger
    
    Learn more: [Rewards Guide](/btc-summer/rewards)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are forfeitable terms?">
    The Bitcoin Summer campaign uses "soft-lock" incentives: Rewards are distributed at the end of each 2-month epoch. You can withdraw at any time, but withdrawing during an epoch forfeits the pro-rata share of rewards accumulated during that epoch.
    
    **Example**: If you deposit 10 BTC and withdraw 5 BTC mid-epoch, you forfeit rewards corresponding to the withdrawn amount.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I use Ethereum vaults or direct Neutron deposits?">
    **Choose Ethereum Path if**:
    - You're comfortable with MetaMask/EVM wallets
    - You want automated strategies with simple deposits
    - You're new to the Neutron ecosystem
    
    **Choose Neutron Path if**:
    - You're familiar with Keplr/Cosmos wallets
    - You want direct control over positions
    - You want immediate access to boost mechanisms
    
    Compare: [Ethereum Path](/btc-summer/ethereum-path) vs [Neutron Path](/btc-summer/neutron-path)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## About maxBTC

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is maxBTC?">
    maxBTC is liquid, yield-bearing Bitcoin that offers real BTC yield through on- and off-chain strategies.
    
    **Key features:**
    - **Real BTC Yield**: Attractive BTC-denominated yield from tested strategies, not emissions
    - **LST-like Form Factor**: Composable design for DeFi integrations
    - **Liquid**: Withdraw anytime or trade via DEXs with minimal slippage
    - **Transparent and Secure**: Clear strategy mechanics and risk metrics
    - **Scalable**: Supports multiple strategies without manual capital rotation
    
    maxBTC is developed by **Structured**. Learn more at [structured.money](https://structured.money/).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does maxBTC generate yield?">
    maxBTC generates yield through tested on- and off-chain strategies. The initial strategy is based on the Jupiter Liquidity Provider (JLP) pool, which backs leveraged positions on Jupiter Perps and earns fees from trader activity.
    
    **Fee sources:**
    - Opening and closing fees on trades
    - Borrowing fees paid by leveraged traders
    - Protocol trading fees
    - Liquidation fees
    
    The strategy allocates ~70% to JLP and ~30% as collateral for hedging, converting trading activity into BTC-denominated yield.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is KYC required to mint and redeem maxBTC?">
    KYC ensures compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) laws and regulations. Because minting and redeeming involve direct interaction with Structured's vaults, verification protects both participants and the protocol.
    
    **KYC options**:
    - **Individuals**: Complete KYC through zkMe with ID and proof of address
    - **Entities**: Manual verification with the Structured team
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I buy maxBTC?">
    maxBTC can be purchased on Neutron's built-in [orderbook](https://app.neutron.org/orderbook) or through campaign vaults that provide maxBTC exposure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## About Protocols

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Amber Protocol?">
    Amber Finance is a BTC-native DeFi platform built on Mars Protocol infrastructure. It lets you put your Bitcoin to work while preserving value and earning yield.
    
    **Available strategies**:
    - **Lend BTC**: Earn passive yield from lending markets
    - **Loop BTC**: Boost exposure and earn higher returns with leverage
    - **Basis trading**: Use BTC as collateral for structured strategies
    - **Borrow against BTC**: Access liquidity without selling
    
    **Access**: [amber.fi/app](https://amber.fi/app) | **Strategies**: [app.amberfi.io/strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Supervaults and how do they work?">
    Supervaults provide institutional-grade automated market making with CEX-level performance.
    
    **Key features**:
    - **Automated LP management**: Optimal position management without manual intervention
    - **Tight spreads**: CEX-grade market-making performance
    - **Minimal adverse selection**: Advanced algorithms protect against MEV
    - **Steady yields**: Earn from trading fees + campaign rewards
    
    **Learn more**: [Supervaults Documentation](/defi/supervaults) | **Access**: [app.neutron.org/supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is leveraged looping and why is it important for BTC?">
    Leverage looping maximizes yield by using a yield-bearing token as collateral to borrow the underlying asset, then converting it back to the yield-bearing token and repeating.
    
    **For Bitcoin**: This strategy was previously impossible because there were no high-yielding Bitcoin tokens. maxBTC (yielding 5-10% APR) is the engine that powers leverage looping for Bitcoin on Neutron.
    
    **How it works on Amber**:
    1. Deposit maxBTC as collateral
    2. Borrow BTC derivatives (eBTC, etc.)
    3. Swap borrowed assets for more maxBTC
    4. Repeat to amplify exposure and yield
    
    Learn more about [Amber strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Rewards & Boosts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do NTRN locking boosts work?">
    1. **Lock NTRN** for up to 4 years to earn substantial reward multipliers
    2. **Receive receipt NFT** representing your locked position
    3. **Point boost** to the address where you deposited BTC
    4. **Earn multiplier** up to 2x boost (3x total rewards)
    5. **Additional yield**: 3% APR on locked NTRN through Drop staking
    
    **Formula**: Boost = min(2, (NTRN_Locked / TVL_Deposited) × (Duration_user / Duration_max) × 2)
    
    Learn more: [Rewards Guide](/btc-summer/rewards)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I boost multiple addresses?">
    **No** - you can only boost 1 address at a time. Updating your boost target automatically moves all previous boosts to the new address.
    
    **Critical rule**: Point boost to the same address where you deposited BTC.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I point my boost to the wrong address?">
    Your boost won't work correctly. Make sure to:
    - **Ethereum deposits**: Point boost to your **EVM address**
    - **Neutron deposits**: Point boost to your **Neutron address**
    - **Update if needed**: You can change your boost target anytime
    
    Technical details: [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are boost NFTs tradeable?">
    Yes, receipt NFTs from NTRN locking are transferable:
    - Can be traded on secondary markets (e.g., [Superbolt](https://superbolt.wtf/))
    - Boost benefits only apply to current holder
    - New holder must stake NFT to activate boost
    - Original holder loses boost benefits upon transfer
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technical Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are rewards calculated?">
    Rewards use a points-based system with daily calculations:
    
    **Formula**: Points = USD Value × Days Staked × (1 + Boost Multiplier)
    
    - **Daily calculations**: Points accrue each day based on USD value
    - **Pro-rata distribution**: Your share = (Your Points / Total Points) × Epoch Budget
    - **Dollar-denominated**: Converted to NTRN at epoch end
    
    Details: [Campaign Details](/btc-summer/campaign-details)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I find my positions after withdrawal?">
    **Depends on your participation path**:
    
    **Simple Lending Vaults** (Ethereum Path):
    - Underlying assets (wBTC, eBTC) sent back to your original Ethereum wallet
    - No additional setup needed
    
    **LP Strategy Vaults** (Ethereum Path):
    - Supervault LP tokens sent to your Neutron wallet
    - Track at [app.neutron.org/balance](https://app.neutron.org/balance)
    
    **Direct Neutron Deposits**:
    - Positions remain in your Neutron wallet
    - Manage through protocol interfaces
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I withdraw early?">
    - **Principal**: Always available (no lock on deposits)
    - **Campaign rewards**: Forfeit pro-rata share for current epoch
    - **Previous epochs**: Completed epoch rewards remain safe
    - **DeFi yields**: Keep any earned protocol yields
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My boost isn't working - what should I check?">
    **Common issues**:
    - Boost pointed to wrong address (must match deposit address)
    - NFT not properly staked to boost contract
    - Conflicting wallet connections (use one primary wallet)
    - Address linking not completed properly
    
    **Fix**: Ensure boost points to the same address where you deposited BTC.
    
    Technical help: [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
