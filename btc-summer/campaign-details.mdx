The Bitcoin Summer campaign is Neutron's comprehensive incentive program designed to bootstrap Bitcoin DeFi liquidity and demonstrate the power of integrated blockchain infrastructure. The campaign distributes up to **$10M in NTRN rewards** over multiple phases to participants who provide liquidity and engage with Neutron's DeFi protocols.

<Info>
For detailed reward and incentive mechanics, see [Rewards](/btc-summer/rewards).
</Info>

## Campaign Structure

### Phases & Timeline

The campaign is structured in successive **2-month phases**, with at least 6 phases planned (12+ months total duration). Each phase has predetermined parameters set before launch:

- **Phase Budget**: Dollar value distributed as incentives (e.g., Phase 1: $250,000)
- **Eligible Opportunities**: List of participating vaults and protocols
- **Reward Multipliers**: Boost mechanisms and qualification criteria

Earlier phases have smaller budgets to account for initial visibility, while later phases scale up to drive broader participation.

### Total Budget & Distribution

- **Total Allocation**: $10M or up to 10% of NTRN supply (whichever is lower)
- **Duration**: 6-12 phases over 12-24 months  
- **Budget Growth**: Later phases receive larger allocations to scale the campaign

## How Rewards Work

The campaign rewards actual **DeFi positions on Neutron**, not just vault deposits. This means:

- **Vault participants** receive pro-rata shares of rewards earned by their vault's underlying positions
- **Direct participants** earn rewards by depositing directly into eligible Neutron protocols
- **Tiered accounting** ensures fair distribution across multiple layers of deposits

Whether you deposit through Ethereum vaults or directly on Neutron, you're earning rewards from the same underlying DeFi positions.

### Dollar-Denominated Rewards

Unlike traditional token farming, rewards are calculated in **dollar terms** throughout each phase and converted to NTRN tokens at phase end based on the final NTRN price.

**Example:**
- Alice deposits 1 BTC ($100,000) at phase start
- Earns $1,660 in rewards over 2 months (~10% APR)
- At phase end, if NTRN = $1, Alice receives ~1,660 NTRN tokens

This structure means campaign costs decrease if NTRN price appreciates, aligning incentives with network growth.

### Points System

Rewards use a points-based accounting system to ensure fair distribution:

$$
\text{Points} = \text{USD Value of Deposit} \times \text{Days Staked} \times (1 + \text{Bonus Multipliers})
$$

- **Daily Calculation**: Points accrue daily based on USD deposit value  
- **Pro-rata Distribution**: Phase budget distributed proportionally to total points earned
- **Bonus Multipliers**: Additional rewards for advanced strategies ([learn more](/btc-summer/rewards))
- **Protection from Dilution**: Daily calculation locks in dollar-denominated rewards

## Reward Claiming & Vesting

After each phase ends, participants have multiple claiming options:

1. **Instant Claim**: Immediate access with 50% penalty
2. **Standard Vesting**: Full rewards vested over 2 months
3. **Enhanced Staking**: Lock rewards for additional benefits ([details](/btc-summer/rewards))

The vesting mechanism promotes long-term ecosystem participation and prevents sudden token supply releases.

## Vault Incentive Model

### Soft-Lock Structure

Campaign vaults use a "forfeitable" reward structure to encourage full phase participation:

- **No Principal Lock**: Users can withdraw deposits at any time
- **Reward Forfeiture**: Early withdrawal forfeits pro-rata share of accrued rewards for that phase
- **Full Phase Participation**: To secure rewards, maintain deposits throughout the entire 2-month phase

**Example**: If you deposit 10 BTC at phase start but withdraw 5 BTC mid-phase, you forfeit rewards corresponding to the withdrawn amount.

### Withdrawal Process

- **Ethereum Vaults**: Withdrawals processed on Neutron side
- **What You Receive**: Supervault LP tokens + any lending assets directly to your wallet
- **Timeline**: Processing depends on underlying protocol liquidity
- **Finding Positions**: Track positions through Neutron interfaces

---

### Participation Requirements
- **Minimum Deposit**: No minimum (gas fees apply)
- **Geographic Restrictions**: Open globally (check local regulations)
- **KYC Requirements**: Required for certain vault strategies only

### Terms & Conditions
- Rewards subject to network participation requirements
- Early withdrawal penalties apply to campaign rewards
- Technical risks associated with smart contract interactions
- Regulatory compliance is user responsibility
