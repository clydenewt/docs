This guide covers the evergreen process for Ethereum participation in BTC Summer. For current Phase 1 specific vault listings and reward budgets, see [Phase 1 Details](/btc-summer/ethereum-path/phase-1).

## How It Works

When you deposit through BTC Summer vaults, your assets are automatically:

1. **Bridged** to Neutron via secure cross-chain infrastructure
2. **Deployed** to eligible DeFi protocols ([Amber](https://amber.fi/app) lending, [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults) LP)
3. **Earning** rewards from both DeFi yields and NTRN campaign incentives

<Info>
All vault withdrawals and campaign rewards are processed on Neutron - you'll need a Neutron wallet to receive both your NTRN campaign rewards and withdrawn assets (Supervault LP tokens, lending assets) regardless of vault strategy. For Neutron wallet setup, see our [User Quickstart Guide](/quickstart#for-users). For detailed cross-chain coordination, see [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking).
</Info>

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Prepare Your Wallet

**Compatible Wallets**:
- MetaMask (recommended)

**Required Assets**: 
- Supported BTC derivatives (wBTC, eBTC, SolvBTC, uniBTC)
- ETH for gas fees
- USDC (for USDC-maxBTC vault only)

### 2. Navigate to Bitcoin Summer

Visit [app.neutron.org/btc-summer](https://app.neutron.org/btc-summer) to access the vault interface.

### 3. Complete Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Vault">
    Choose a vault from the available options
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Ethereum Wallet">
    Connect your Ethereum wallet when prompted at the bottom of the deposit modal
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Deposit Amount">
    Enter amount or click "Use Max" to deposit your entire balance
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Click the "Deposit" button and approve the transaction in your wallet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Position">
    Track your position on the [Balance page](https://app.neutron.org/balance) or Bitcoin Summer interface
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 4. Monitor Rewards

- **Campaign Rewards**: NTRN incentives based on your deposit size and duration
- **DeFi Yields**: Underlying protocol rewards (lending rates, LP fees)
- **Asset Issuer Rewards**: Potential additional rewards from LST issuers

## Withdrawal Process

<Info>
**Important**: Withdrawals are processed on the Neutron side. You'll receive the underlying positions directly to your wallet, not back through the vault interface.
</Info>

**Need to set up a Neutron wallet?** See our [User Quickstart Guide](/quickstart#for-users) for step-by-step wallet setup instructions.


### What You Receive
**Simple Lending Vaults** (Just Lend):
- **Underlying assets** (wBTC, eBTC, etc.) sent to your Neutron wallet
- **Campaign rewards** (NTRN tokens) sent to your Neutron wallet

**LP Strategy Vaults** (Hybrid, LST Main, maxBTC):
- **Supervault LP tokens** (Neutron-native tokens)
- **Lending assets** (underlying BTC derivatives)
- **Campaign rewards** (NTRN tokens)
- **All sent to your Neutron wallet**

Your positions can be tracked on the [Balance page of the Neutron App](https://app.neutron.org/balance)

## Maximize Your Rewards

<Note>
**Want to boost your rewards up to 3x?** You'll need to set up a Neutron wallet and lock NTRN tokens, then point the boost to your EVM deposit address.
</Note>

### Address Linking for Boosts

**Important**: If you deposited using your **EVM address**, you must boost that **same EVM address**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up Neutron Wallet">
    Set up a Neutron wallet (Keplr or Leap recommended). See our [User Quickstart Guide](/quickstart#for-users) for step-by-step wallet setup instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acquire NTRN Tokens">
    Get NTRN tokens through exchanges, swaps, or bridges
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Booster Feature">
    Use the "Unlock your booster" feature on the Bitcoin Summer page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link Your Address">
    Select "Link an address" to specify your EVM deposit address
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock NTRN">
    Lock NTRN and earn up to 3x total rewards
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Status">
    Monitor your boost status on the [Balance page](https://app.neutron.org/balance)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
For complete boost strategies, NTRN locking mechanics, and detailed guidance, see [Rewards Guide](/btc-summer/rewards). For technical implementation details, see [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking).
</Info>
