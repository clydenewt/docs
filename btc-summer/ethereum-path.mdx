## How It Works

When you deposit through Ethereum vaults, your assets are automatically:

1. **Bridged** to Neutron via secure cross-chain infrastructure
2. **Deployed** to eligible DeFi protocols ([Amber](https://amber.fi/app) lending, [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults) LP)
3. **Earning** rewards from both DeFi yields and NTRN campaign incentives

<Info>
Simple lending vaults and rewards can be fully managed through an EVM wallet, whereas LP strategies require a Neutron wallet to withdraw LP tokens. For detailed cross-chain coordination, see [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking).
</Info>

## Available Vault Categories

### Simple Lending Vaults
**Best for**: Risk-averse users wanting straightforward yield and management fully through EVM wallets (deposit and withdraw)

- **Just Lend - wBTC on Amber**: 100% lending on [Amber Protocol](https://amber.fi/app)
- **Just Lend - wBTC on Mars**: 100% lending on Mars Protocol
- **Requirements**: No KYC, no deposit caps
- **Strategy**: Pure lending exposure with stable yields

### Hybrid Strategy Vaults  
**Best for**: Users wanting diversified exposure

- **wBTC with LST LP**: Combines lending with liquidity provision
- **Available pairs**: eBTC, SolvBTC, uniBTC
- **Strategy**: Split between [Amber](https://amber.fi/app) lending and [Supervault](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults) LP positions
- **Requirements**: No KYC required

### maxBTC Access Vaults
**Best for**: Users wanting exposure to maxBTC ecosystem

- **MaxBTC Liquidity Vault**: Provides liquidity to maxBTC-wBTC pool (**KYC required**)
- **MaxBTC Deposit Vault**: Direct maxBTC holding strategy (**KYC required**)
- **Just LP - USDC maxBTC**: USDC deposits to maxBTC-USDC pool (**No KYC required**)
- **Requirements**: KYC only required for MaxBTC Liquidity and MaxBTC Deposit vaults

### LST Main Vaults
**Best for**: LST holders wanting maxBTC exposure

- **Available LSTs**: eBTC, SolvBTC, uniBTC
- **Strategy**: Lend LST + provide liquidity to maxBTC-LST pools
- **Requirements**: No KYC, direct LST deposits

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Prepare Your Wallet

**Compatible Wallets**:
- MetaMask (recommended)
- Coinbase Wallet
- WalletConnect compatible wallets

**Required Assets**: 
- Supported BTC derivatives (wBTC, eBTC, SolvBTC, uniBTC)
- ETH for gas fees
- USDC (for USDC-maxBTC vault only)

<Info>
**For LP Strategy Withdrawals**: You'll need a Neutron wallet (Keplr recommended) to receive Supervault LP tokens. Simple lending vaults can withdraw to your original wallet.
</Info>

### 2. Navigate to Bitcoin Summer

Visit [app.neutron.org/btc-summer](https://app.neutron.org/btc-summer) to access the vault interface.

**Supported assets**: WBTC, eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC, and USDC

**Selection criteria**:
- **Asset type**: What BTC derivatives you currently hold
- **Risk tolerance**: Simple lending vs LP strategies
- **KYC willingness**: Only maxBTC vaults require KYC
- **Reward goals**: Different strategies offer different yield profiles

### 3. Complete Deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Vault">
    Choose a vault from the available options (note: some may be in beta)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Ethereum Wallet">
    Connect your Ethereum wallet when prompted at the bottom of the deposit modal
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Deposit Amount">
    Enter amount or click "Use Max" to deposit your entire balance
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Click the "Deposit" button and approve the transaction in your wallet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Position">
    Track your position on the [Balance page](https://app.neutron.org/balance) or Bitcoin Summer interface
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 4. Monitor Rewards

- **Campaign Rewards**: NTRN incentives based on your deposit size and duration
- **DeFi Yields**: Underlying protocol rewards (lending rates, LP fees)
- **Asset Issuer Rewards**: Potential additional rewards from LST issuers

## Withdrawal Process

<Warning>
**Important**: Withdrawals are processed on the Neutron side. You'll receive the underlying positions directly to your wallet, not back through the vault interface.
</Warning>

### What You Receive

**Depending on your vault strategy**:

**Simple Lending Vaults** (Just Lend):
- **Underlying assets** (wBTC, eBTC, etc.) sent back to your original wallet
- **No Neutron wallet needed** for pure lending strategies

**LP Strategy Vaults** (Hybrid, LST Main, maxBTC):
- **Supervault LP tokens** (Neutron-native tokens)
- **Lending assets** (underlying BTC derivatives)
- **Neutron wallet required** to receive LP tokens

### How to Find Your Positions

**Depends on your vault strategy**:

**Simple Lending Vaults**:
- **Underlying assets** (wBTC, eBTC) can be withdrawn back to your original Ethereum wallet
- **No additional setup needed** for pure lending strategies

**LP Strategy Vaults**:
- **Supervault LP tokens** are sent to your Neutron wallet (Neutron-native tokens)
- **Lending portions** may be sent as underlying assets
- **Balance page**: View positions at [app.neutron.org/balance](https://app.neutron.org/balance)
- **Neutron interfaces**: Manage LP positions through Neutron DeFi apps

### Timing Considerations

- **No Principal Lock**: Withdraw deposits anytime
- **Reward Forfeiture**: Early withdrawal forfeits pro-rata epoch rewards
- **Processing Time**: Depends on underlying protocol liquidity
- **Cross-chain delay**: Allow time for bridging and position deployment

## Maximize Your Rewards

<Note>
**Want to boost your rewards up to 3x?** You'll need to set up a Neutron wallet and lock NTRN tokens, then point the boost to your EVM deposit address. This requires cross-chain coordination but can significantly increase your earnings.
</Note>

### Address Linking for Boosts

**Critical Rule**: If you deposited using your **EVM address**, you must boost that **same EVM address**.

**Quick process**:
1. Set up a Neutron wallet (Keplr recommended)
2. Acquire NTRN tokens  
3. Use the "Unlock your booster" feature on the Bitcoin Summer page
4. **Important**: Select "Boost this wallet" if using the same EVM wallet, or "Link an address" to specify your EVM deposit address
5. Lock NTRN and earn up to 3x total rewards

### Boost Coordination

Since boosts require a Neutron wallet but your deposits are on Ethereum:
- You'll connect both wallets during the boost process
- The system will help you link your EVM deposit address to your Neutron boost
- Monitor your boost status on the [Balance page](https://app.neutron.org/balance)

<Info>
For complete boost strategies, NTRN locking mechanics, and detailed guidance, see [Rewards Guide](/btc-summer/rewards). For technical implementation details, see [Address Linking System](/btc-summer/technical/address-linking).
</Info>
