**Best for**: Users familiar with Keplr or Leap wallets who want direct protocol interaction, advanced boost mechanisms, and maximum control over their DeFi positions.

To participate directly from Neutron, users don't need to deposit to vaults. Instead, users deposit directly to eligible DeFi positions on [Amber](https://amber.fi/app) or [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults).

<Info>
This guide covers the evergreen process for direct Neutron participation. For current Phase 1 specific protocol eligibility and reward multipliers, see [Phase 1 Details](/btc-summer/neutron-path/phase-1).
</Info>

## Why Choose Direct Participation?

### Advantages
- **Immediate boost access**: No cross-chain coordination needed
- **Direct control**: Manage positions directly through protocol interfaces  
- **Advanced features**: Access to all boost mechanisms and strategies
- **Higher potential rewards**: Native access to multiplier systems
- **No intermediary fees**: Direct protocol interaction

### Requirements
- Neutron-compatible wallet (Keplr, Leap)
- Basic understanding of DeFi protocols
- Comfort with direct protocol interaction

## Wallet Setup

### 1. Install Compatible Wallet

**Recommended**: [Keplr Wallet](https://www.keplr.app/) or [Leap Wallet](https://www.leapwallet.io/)
- Browser extension and mobile app available
- Native Neutron support
- Integrated with all campaign features


### 2. Connect to Neutron

1. Navigate to [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org)
2. Click **Connect** in the top-right corner
3. Select your Neutron wallet (Keplr/Leap)
4. Network will be added automatically if not present

### 3. Fund Your Account

**Get NTRN for gas fees**:
- Bridge from other Cosmos chains
- Use [app.neutron.org/bridge](https://app.neutron.org/bridge)
- Acquire from exchanges supporting Neutron

**Get campaign assets**:
- Bridge BTC derivatives from Ethereum
- Use native Neutron protocols to acquire positions

## Available Protocols

### Amber Protocol (AmberFi)
**Focus**: Leveraged looping and basis trading strategies

**What it offers**:
- Lending markets for BTC derivatives
- Leveraged positions up to 10x
- Basis trading with maxBTC collateral
- Institutional-grade risk management

**Access Points**:
- **Deposit page**: [amber.fi/app](https://amber.fi/app)
- **Strategies page**: [app.amberfi.io/strategies](https://app.amberfi.io/strategies) (leveraged looping and basis trading)
- **Alternative access**: Via [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org) Earn V2 → All tab → Amber positions

**Campaign eligibility**: Direct lending and leveraged positions earn NTRN rewards

### Supervaults
**Focus**: Automated market making with institutional performance

**What it offers**:
- Automated LP position management
- Tight spreads and minimal adverse selection
- CEX-grade market-making performance
- Steady yield from trading fees
- Institutional-grade automated market making

**How it works**:
- Deposit assets to automated LP strategies  
- Vaults manage positions with optimal parameters
- Earn from trading fees + NTRN campaign rewards
- Automated rebalancing and optimization

**Access**:
- **Direct interface**: [app.neutron.org/supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults)
- **Documentation**: [Supervaults Guide](/defi/supervaults)

**Campaign eligibility**: All BTC derivative Supervault positions earn NTRN rewards

## Direct Deposit Process

### For Amber Protocol

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Amber">
    Navigate to [amber.fi/app](https://amber.fi/app) OR from [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org) Earn V2 → **All** tab → scroll to Amber lending positions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Wallet">
    Connect your Neutron wallet to the Amber interface
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Asset">
    Choose from available BTC derivatives (wBTC, uniBTC, eBTC, solvBTC)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Strategy">
    Select lending or leveraged strategies based on your goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Deposit">
    Execute transaction and confirm in your wallet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Rewards">
    Track both Amber yields and Bitcoin Summer campaign points
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For Supervaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Earn V2">
    From [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org), click **Earn V2** tab in the left column
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Supervaults Tab">
    Click on the **Supervaults** tab to see Bitcoin Summer eligible vaults
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose BTC Pool">
    Select from available BTC derivative pools (marked for Bitcoin Summer campaign)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit Assets">
    Make single-sided or double-sided deposits (single-sided for maxBTC pairs)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Press Deposit, Confirm, and sign the wallet transaction
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Position">
    View "My Position TVL" section that appears below the chart
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Advanced Boost Opportunities

### NTRN Locking (Native Access)

**Advantage**: Direct access without cross-chain coordination

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Boost">
    From Earn V2 landing page, select "Unlock your booster"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Boost Target">
    Since you deposited with your Neutron address, select "Boost this wallet" (boosts your connected Neutron wallet)
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Boost Overview">
    Select "View boost overview" to see current multiplier options
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock NTRN">
    Select "Stake & Lock" to lock NTRN or dNTRN for up to 4 years
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Lock">
    Pick lock asset (NTRN or dNTRN), duration, and amount
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Lock">
    Select "Lock" and sign the transaction
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Address Linking Simplified Guidance

**Most Important**: If you deposited with your Neutron address, you should boost that **same Neutron address**, not any other address.

**Why this matters**:
- **Consistency**: Boosts only work when pointed to the address with deposits
- **Simplicity**: No cross-chain coordination needed for Neutron users
- **Immediate activation**: Direct boost access without complexity
- **Native advantage**: Can simply "Boost this wallet" during the locking process

## Reward Optimization

### Maximize Your Position

1. **Start with core deposits**: Establish positions in [Amber](https://amber.fi/app) or [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults)
2. **Add NTRN locking**: Boost your rewards with locked NTRN
3. **Monitor performance**: Track both DeFi yields and campaign rewards
4. **Adjust strategy**: Optimize based on changing market conditions

## Comparing Strategies

### When to Choose [Amber](https://amber.fi/app)
- **Good for**: Users wanting leveraged exposure
- **Risk level**: Higher (leverage, liquidation risk)
- **Reward potential**: High (leverage multiplies rewards)
- **Best when**: Borrowing costs are low, yields are high

### When to Choose [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults)
- **Good for**: Users wanting steady, automated yields
- **Risk level**: Moderate (impermanent loss, market making)
- **Reward potential**: Steady (trading fees + campaign rewards)
- **Best when**: High trading volume, stable market conditions

<Note>
These are general considerations, not financial advice (NFA). Always do your own research and understand the risks.
</Note>
