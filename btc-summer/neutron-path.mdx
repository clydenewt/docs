**Best for**: Users familiar with Keplr or Leap wallets who want direct protocol interaction, advanced boost mechanisms, and maximum control over their DeFi positions.

To participate directly from Neutron, users don't need to deposit to vaults. Instead, users deposit directly to eligible DeFi positions on [AmberFi](https://app.amberfi.io/) ([docs](https://docs.amberfi.io/)) or [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/supervaults).

<Info>
This guide covers the evergreen process for direct Neutron participation. For current Phase 1 specific protocol eligibility and reward multipliers, see [Phase 1 Details](/btc-summer/neutron-path/phase-1).
</Info>

## Why Choose Direct Participation?

### Advantages
- **Immediate boost access**: No cross-chain coordination needed
- **Direct control**: Manage positions directly through protocol interfaces  
- **No intermediary fees**

### Requirements
- Neutron-compatible wallet (Keplr, Leap)
- Basic understanding of DeFi protocols
- Comfort with direct protocol interaction

<Info>
**New to Neutron?** See our [User Quickstart Guide](/quickstart#for-users) for comprehensive wallet setup and onboarding instructions. 
</Info>

## Direct Deposit Process

### For Amber Protocol

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Amber">
    Navigate to [app.amberfi.io](https://app.amberfi.io/) OR from [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org) Earn V2 → **All** tab → scroll to Amber lending positions
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Wallet">
    Connect your Neutron wallet to the Amber interface
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Asset">
    Choose from available BTC derivatives (wBTC, uniBTC, eBTC, solvBTC)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Strategy">
    Select lending or leveraged strategies based on your goals
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Deposit">
    Execute transaction and confirm in your wallet
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Rewards">
    Track both BTC yields and Bitcoin Summer campaign points. Activities earn 1x-6x NTRN multipliers - see [NTRN Rewards Multipliers](/btc-summer/rewards#ntrn-rewards-multipliers)
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For Supervaults

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Earn V2">
    From [app.neutron.org](https://app.neutron.org), click **Earn V2** tab in the left column
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Supervaults Tab">
    Click on the **Supervaults** tab to see Bitcoin Summer eligible Supervaults
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose BTC Pool">
    Select from available BTC derivative pools (marked for Bitcoin Summer campaign)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit Assets">
    Make single-sided or double-sided deposits
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Transaction">
    Press Deposit, Confirm, and sign the wallet transaction
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor Position">
    View "My Position TVL" section that appears below the chart
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Advanced Boost Opportunities

### NTRN Staking (Native Access)

**Advantage**: Direct access without cross-chain coordination

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Boost">
    From Earn V2 landing page, select "Unlock your booster"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Boost Target">
    Since you deposited with your Neutron address, select "Boost this wallet" (boosts your connected Neutron wallet)
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Boost Overview">
    Select "View boost overview" to see current multiplier options
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake NTRN">
    Select "Stake & Lock" to stake NTRN or dNTRN for up to 4 years
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Stake">
    Pick stake asset (NTRN or dNTRN), duration, and amount
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Stake">
    Select "Stake" and sign the transaction
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
If you deposited with your Neutron address, you should boost that **same Neutron address**, not any other address.
</Warning>
