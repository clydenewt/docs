## What Was New in Phase 2

Phase 2 introduced meaningful upgrades to vault mechanics and reward structures.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="wBTC Lending Multiplier Increased to 4x">
    wBTC remains the backbone of on-chain Bitcoin liquidity. The wBTC lending multiplier on Amber increased from 3x to 4x.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Simpler, More Accessible Boosting">
    The Boost mechanism received a major upgrade. Max boost became achievable with just a one-year lock (previously 4 years).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Updated Looping Incentives">
    Amber's looping vaults had an updated reward structure where incentives were distributed based on collateral minus debt rather than on the full notional amount leveraged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ethereum Vaults Sunset & Neutron-Native maxBTC">
    Phase 2 completed the full transition to Neutron. maxBTC became mintable directly on Neutron.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Phase 2 Eligible Protocols

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Amber Protocol (AmberFi)">
    #### wBTC Lending on Amber
    **Eligible positions**: wBTC lending positions  
    **Reward multiplier**: 4x base

    #### BTC LST Lending on Amber  
    **Eligible positions**: BTC LST lending (eBTC, solvBTC, uniBTC)  
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x base

    #### Looping Strategies on Amber
    **Eligible positions**: maxBTC looping with wBTC, uniBTC, solvBTC, eBTC  
    **Reward calculation**: Based on collateral minus debt  
    **Reward multiplier**: 1x base
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Supervaults">
    #### maxBTC Supervaults
    **Eligible positions**: maxBTC paired with various assets (wBTC, USDC, LSTs)  
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x-6x depending on pair

    #### BTC LST Supervaults
    **Eligible positions**: BTC LST pairs  
    **Reward multiplier**: 2x base
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Mars Protocol">
    **Eligible positions**: wBTC lending positions
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Astroport DEX">
    **Eligible positions**: Various BTC asset LP pairs including USDC/wBTC, maxBTC/wBTC, USDC/maxBTC, and various LST pairs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
