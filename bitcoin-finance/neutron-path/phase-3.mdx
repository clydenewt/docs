## What's New in Phase 3

Phase 3 continues to refine the reward structure and eligible positions, focusing incentives on core strategies.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Looping Incentives Increased to 2x">
    Leverage looping positions on Amber have been increased from 1x to **2x** (on equity/collateral minus debt). This makes looping one of the most attractive strategies for users seeking amplified yield.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Streamlined Eligible Positions">
    Phase 3 focuses rewards on the most impactful positions:
    
    - **Astroport**: Only wBTC/maxBTC and maxBTC/USDC LP pools remain eligible
    - **Mars Protocol**: wBTC lending has been removed from eligible positions
    - **Core strategies**: Supervaults and Amber remain the primary eligible protocols
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Simplified LST Multipliers">
    BTC LST positions have been consolidated to 1x base multiplier:
    
    - LST lending on Amber (uniBTC, solvBTC, eBTC): Now 1x
    - LST Supervault LPs: Now 1x
    
    This simplifies participation while maintaining rewards for core strategies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Phase 3 Boost Mechanics

### Max Boost with 1-Year Lock

The boost system remains the same as Phase 2:

| Lock Duration | Max Boost Multiplier |
| ------------- | -------------------- |
| 1 year | 3x |

**Boost Formula:**
$$
\text{Boost} = \min(2, \frac{\text{NTRN}_{\text{Locked}}}{\text{TVL}_{\text{Deposited}}} \times \frac{\text{Duration}_{\text{user}}}{\text{Duration}_{\text{max (1 year)}}} \times 2)
$$

This means:
- **New participants** can achieve max boost with a 1-year lock
- **Existing lockers** with locks ≥1 year already qualify for max boost
- You continue earning staking rewards (~3% APR) on locked NTRN
