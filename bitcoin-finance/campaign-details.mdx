## Campaign Structure

### Phases & Timeline

The campaign is structured in successive **2-month phases**, with at least 6 phases planned (12+ months total duration). Each phase has predetermined parameters set before launch:

- **Phase Budget**: Dollar value distributed as incentives (e.g., Phase 3: $300,000 in NTRN Rewards)
- **Eligible Opportunities**: List of participating positions and protocols
- **NTRN Reward Multipliers**: Boost mechanisms and qualification criteria

Earlier phases have smaller budgets to account for initial visibility, while later phases scale up to drive broader participation.

## How Rewards Work

The campaign rewards actual **DeFi positions on Neutron**. Participants earn rewards by depositing directly into eligible Neutron protocols ([Amber](https://app.amberfi.io/) lending or [Supervaults](https://app.neutron.org/earn/supervaults) LP).

### Dollar-Denominated Rewards

Unlike traditional token farming, rewards are calculated in **dollar terms** throughout each phase and converted to NTRN tokens at phase end based on the final NTRN price.

**Example:**
- Alice deposits 1 BTC ($100,000) at phase start
- Earns $1,660 in rewards over 2 months (~10% APR)
- At phase end, if NTRN = $1, Alice receives ~1,660 NTRN tokens

This structure means campaign costs decrease if NTRN price appreciates, aligning incentives with network growth.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Points System">
    Rewards use a points-based accounting system to ensure fair distribution:

    $$
    \text{Points} = \text{USD Value of Deposit} \times \text{Days Staked} \times (1 + \text{Bonus Multipliers})
    $$

    - **Daily Calculation**: Points accrue daily based on USD deposit value  
    - **Pro-rata Distribution**: Phase budget distributed proportionally to total points earned
    - **Bonus Multipliers**: Additional rewards for advanced strategies ([learn more](/bitcoin-finance/rewards#understanding-multipliers))
    - **Protection from Dilution**: Daily calculation locks in dollar-denominated rewards
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reward Claiming & Vesting

After each BTCFi phase ends, participants have three options to claim NTRN rewards:

1. **Boost & Earn Staking Rewards**: Stake and lock instantly to boost NTRN rewards in upcoming phases by up to 3x and earn NTRN staking rewards
2. **Instant Claim**: Instantly claim 50% and forfeit the other 50%
3. **Standard Vesting**: Vest over 2 months to claim the full amount

The vesting mechanism promotes long-term ecosystem participation and prevents sudden token supply releases.

## Forfeitable Rewards Structure

BTCFi on Neutron uses a "forfeitable" reward structure designed to balance liquidity with participation incentives:

| Component | Terms | Details |
| --------- | ----- | ------- |
| **Principal** | No lockup | Your principal remains fully liquid at all times. |
| **DeFi Rewards** | No lockup | Lending yields and swap fees are automatically compounded into your position and can be withdrawn at any time. |
| **NTRN Rewards** | Forfeitable | Withdrawing mid-Phase forfeits that Phase's NTRN rewards proportionally. |
| **Partner Rewards** | No lockup by default | BTC LST issuers generally do not enforce forfeitable incentives. Check with specific asset issuers to confirm their approach. |

**Key Mechanic**: Users can withdraw deposits at any time, but NTRN rewards are forfeited proportionally to withdrawals made before phase completion.

**Example**: If you withdraw 50% of your deposit halfway through a phase, you'll forfeit 50% of the NTRN rewards for that phase. Your principal, DeFi yields, and partner rewards remain fully accessible.

For detailed reward optimization strategies and worked examples: [NTRN Reward Structure & Examples](/bitcoin-finance/rewards#forfeitable-reward-structure)

Learn more about NTRN multipliers in [Boost Mechanisms & Multipliers](/bitcoin-finance/rewards#understanding-multipliers)
